E61 Group Head Leaking? The Three Leaks and How to Fix Each

Applies to Lelit Bianca & Mara X, ECM, Profitec, Bezzera, Rocket, and any machine with an E61 brew group

  • First, Find Which Leak You Have

    Run a shot with a flashlight on the group. Drips at the portafilter rim = worn group gasket — the 20-minute fix. Weeping from under the group between shots = the internal mushroom/cam valves need service. Leaking at the brew lever pivot = cam o-rings. The E61 is a 60-year-old design precisely because all three are serviceable.

  • The Gasket Fix (Most Common)

    Group gaskets harden from heat cycles and should be replaced every 12–24 months. If your portafilter swings far past 6 o'clock to seal, the gasket is due. Pop the shower screen, pick the old gasket out, seat the new one, backflush.

  • Weeping and Lever Leaks: the E61 Overhaul

    The mushroom valve and cam assembly need periodic cleaning, descaling, new o-rings, and food-safe lubrication.
